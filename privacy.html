Privacy

The game stores your board, preferences, personal best and optional player name in your browser so you can continue playing.

If you submit a score, the player name, score and submission time are stored in the public leaderboard. Do not use sensitive information as your player name.

Optional Google Analytics is loaded only after you select “Allow analytics”. Analytics is configured without advertising signals. You can clear the site’s local storage in your browser to reset your choice and saved game.

No account is required to play.
